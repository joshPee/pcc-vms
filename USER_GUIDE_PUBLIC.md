## Event Registration

### Step 1: Access the Registration Page

Navigate to the registration page for the active event. The page will display:
- Event name
- Event date
- Registration status (open/closed)

### Step 2: Complete the Registration Form

Fill out the required information:

**Full Name** (Required)
- Enter your complete legal name
- Minimum 2 characters
- Numbers only are not allowed

**Organisation** (Required)
- Enter the name of your organization or company
- Minimum 2 characters
- Numbers only are not allowed

**Position** (Required)
- Enter your job title or position
- Minimum 2 characters
- Numbers only are not allowed

### Step 3: Submit Your Registration

Click the "Register" button to submit your information.

**Important Notes:**
- If a similar registration already exists, you'll see a warning
- You can choose to:
  - View your existing registration code
  - Submit a new registration anyway
  - Cancel and modify your information

## Receiving Your Registration Code

After successful registration, you will receive:
- A unique registration code
- Your name confirmation
- Instructions to save this code

**What to do with your code:**
- Save it in a secure location (phone notes, screenshot, or write it down)
- Bring this code with you to the event
- Present it at the registration desk upon arrival for check-in

## Check-In Process

### On Arrival

1. **Locate the Registration Desk**
   - Find the designated check-in area at the venue
   - Have your registration code ready

2. **Present Your Code**
   - Show your registration code to the registration staff
   - Staff will verify your information in the system

3. **Receive Confirmation**
   - Once verified, you'll be marked as "Checked In"
   - You'll receive any event materials or badges

### Walk-In Registration

If you haven't pre-registered:

1. **Visit the Registration Desk**
   - Inform staff you need to register as a walk-in

2. **Provide Your Information**
   - Staff will collect your name, organization, and position
   - You'll receive a registration code immediately

3. **Complete Check-In**
   - Your registration will be processed and you'll be checked in automatically

## Troubleshooting

### Registration Issues

**"Registration Closed" Message**
- This means the event is no longer accepting new registrations
- Contact event organizers if you believe this is an error

**Duplicate Registration Warning**
- If you see this warning, you likely already registered
- Choose "View Existing Registration" to see your code
- If you need a new registration, select "Submit Anyway"

**Form Validation Errors**
- Ensure all fields have at least 2 characters
- Avoid using numbers only in name/organization fields
- Check that all required fields are completed

### Check-In Issues

**Lost Registration Code**
- Visit the registration desk
- Provide your name and organization
- Staff can look up your registration in the system

**"Already Checked In" Message**
- This means you've already been checked in
- If this is incorrect, speak with registration staff

## Tips for a Smooth Experience

1. **Register Early**
   - Complete your registration before the event
   - This saves time at check-in

2. **Save Your Code**
   - Take a screenshot of your registration code
   - Save it to your phone or write it down

3. **Arrive Prepared**
   - Have your registration code ready
   - Bring identification if requested

4. **Contact Support**
   - If you encounter issues, contact event organizers
   - Have your registration details ready for reference
